# Complete Knowledge Pack: Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia Thesis Writing Style (Gaya UKM 2015)

This knowledge base encapsulates all regulations, mechanical dimensions, editorial conventions, and citation rules from the official *Gaya UKM (2015)* e-book published by Pusat Pengajian Siswazah (UKM Graduate Centre).

---

## 1. Physical and Technical Specifications

### Paper and Printing
- **Size**: Standard A4 (`210 mm × 297 mm`), variation after binding must not exceed `2 mm`.
- **Quality**: Minimum `80 gsm` wood-free simile paper for original submitted copies.
- **Printing**: Single-sided only. English/Romanized text printed on right side.

### Typography
- **Typeface**: Times New Roman throughout the thesis.
- **Sizes**:
  - Regular text: `12 pt`
  - Chapter headings & all subheadings: `11 pt`, bold
  - Table titles, table internal text, figure titles, footnotes: `10 pt`
  - Cover & spine: `18 pt`, all CAPITALS, embossed in gold lettering

### Margins
- **Top Margin**: `30 mm` from paper edge
- **Bottom Margin**: `25 mm` from paper edge
- **Left Margin (Binding Side)**: `38 mm` from paper edge
- **Right Margin**: `25 mm` from paper edge
- **Special Drop for Chapter Openings**: Heading `CHAPTER I` must start **`50 mm`** from top edge.
- **Special Margins for Front Matter**: Centered, **`30 mm`** from top edge.

### Line Spacing
- **1.5 Line Spacing**: All regular body text.
- **1.0 Single Line Spacing**: Declarations, Acknowledgements, Malay Abstract (`Abstrak`), English Abstract (`Abstract`), Table of Contents, Lists of Tables/Illustrations, Table cells, Block Quotes (>40 words), Footnotes, and References.

### Paragraph Formatting
- **First paragraph under any heading**: Flush left (`0 mm` indent).
- **Second and subsequent paragraphs**: First line indented by **`12.7 mm` (0.5 inches)**.
- **Widows/Orphans**: The last paragraph on a page must have at least 2 full lines of text; otherwise, carry to the next page.
- **Hyphenation**: The last word on a page must never end with a hyphen.

### Pagination
- **Position**: Top-right corner, `15 mm` from top edge, last digit `25 mm` from right edge.
- **Preliminary Pages**: Lower-case Roman numerals (`i, ii, iii...`). Title page counts as `i` but number is hidden.
- **Body Text**: Arabic numerals (`1, 2, 3...`). First page of each chapter counts but number is hidden.

---

## 2. Mandatory Structural Layout Sequence

The thesis must assemble components strictly in this order:
1. **TITLE PAGE**: Inverted pyramid title `60 mm` from top; submission year `40 mm` from bottom.
2. **TRANSLATION OF TITLE PAGE**: Title page in Bahasa Melayu (for English thesis).
3. **DECLARATION**: Originality statement with signature, date, and matric number.
4. **ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS**: Single-spaced, centered `30 mm` from top.
5. **ABSTRAK**: Malay abstract, 200–350 words, single-spaced, exactly ONE single paragraph.
6. **ABSTRACT**: English abstract, 200–350 words, single-spaced, exactly ONE single paragraph.
7. **CONTENTS**: Table of contents, showing headings down to level 3 (`1.1.1`) only.
8. **LIST OF TABLES**: Chapter-based numbering (e.g. Table 4.1).
9. **LIST OF ILLUSTRATIONS**: Figures, charts, maps, diagrams.
10. **LIST OF SYMBOLS / ABBREVIATIONS / GLOSSARY**
11. **LIST OF CASES / LIST OF STATUTES** (if applicable)
12. **TEXT**: Chapters I to V/VI.
13. **REFERENCES**: Author-Date system, hanging indent `12.5 mm`.
14. **APPENDICES**: APPENDIX A, B, etc.

---

## 3. Heading Hierarchy System

UKM permits a maximum of 5 subtopic tiers. Only levels 1 to 3 may appear in the Table of Contents:
- **Level 1**: `CHAPTER I` (Centered, bold, 11 pt, 50 mm from top) -> Chapter title 2 spaces below.
- **Level 2**: `1.1  BACKGROUND` (Flush left, bold, 11 pt)
- **Level 3**: `1.1.1  Problem Context` (Flush left, bold, 11 pt)
- **Level 4**: `a.  Market Forces` (Flush left, 11 pt, NOT in TOC)
- **Level 5**: `i.  Pricing Pressures` (Flush left, 11 pt, NOT in TOC)

---

## 4. Tables, Figures & Quotations

### Tables
- Title positioned **ABOVE** the table: `Table 4.1  Demographic Profile of Respondents` (10 pt).
- Classic **three-line table** (Top border, column heading border, bottom border). No vertical gridlines.
- Landscape table: top of table aligned along bound (left) edge.
- Multi-page table: repeat header with `Table 4.1 (continued)`.

### Figures / Illustrations
- Title positioned **BELOW** the figure: `Figure 2.1  Conceptual Framework` (10 pt).
- In-text referral must precede the figure: `...as depicted in Figure 2.1`.

### Quotations
- $\le 40$ words: within body paragraph in double quotes `"..."`.
- $> 40$ words: standalone block, indented `12.7 mm`, single-spaced, NO quotation marks.
- Foreign language quotation: italicized.

## 5. In-Text Citations (Author-Date System)

### Essential Rules
1. **NO COMMA between author and year in parentheses**:
   - Correct: `(Reid 2012)`
   - Prohibited: `(Reid, 2012)`
2. **Two Authors**:
   - Narrative in text: `Abdul Razak and Abu Osman (2009)`
   - Parenthetical: `(Abdul Rahman & Arsmah 2009)`
3. **Three or More Authors**:
   - First author + `et al.`: `Shafaratul et al. (2011)` or `(Siti Balkis et al. 2011)`
4. **Multiple Citations**:
   - Sorted alphabetically by first author, separated by semicolons:
     `(Abdul Samad 2011; Azahan 2011; Jamaluddin 2010; Kadir 2012)`
5. **Same Author Multiple Publications**:
   - Multiple years: `(Jamaluddin 2007, 2009, 2012)`
   - Same year: `(Sham 2010a, 2010b)`
6. **Unknown Author**: `Anon. (2012)` or `(Anon. 2012)`

## 6. List of References Standards

### Typesetting
- Hanging indent: `12.5 mm`. Single-spaced within entry, one blank line between entries.
- Sorted strictly alphabetically A-Z (`Al-Quran` at top).
- **NO PARENTHESES around publication year**: `Author. Year.` (e.g., `Cohen, J. 2009.`).

### Name Formatting
- **Western**: `Surname, Initials.` (e.g. `Bowen, H.J.M.`)
- **Malay**: Direct full name (`Noor Azlan Ghazali. 2012.`) or initials after (`Zakri, A.H.`)
- **Chinese**: Surname first (`Ding, Choo Ming. 2008.` or `Lim, E.H.T. 2005.`)
- **Indian**: Surname or initials after (`Bhaskaran, S. 2010.`)
- **Arabic**: Family name first (`al-Tabrani, Sulayman ibn Ahmad.`)

### Key Entry Formats
- **Journal**:
  `Author. Year. Article title. Journal Name(Italics) Volume(Issue): Pages.`
  *Example*:
  `Samad, S.A. 2004. Digital domain design of cascaded wave digital filters with tunable parameters. Iranian Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering 3(2): 1-5.`
- **Book**:
  `Author. Year. Book Title(Italics). Edition. Place: Publisher.`
  *Example*:
  `Cohen, J. 2009. Statistical Power Analysis for the Behavioral Sciences. Revised Ed. New York: Academic Press.`
- **Chapter in Book**:
  `Author. Year. Chapter title. In Editor (ed.). Book Title(Italics), pp. Pages. Place: Publisher.`
  *Example*:
  `Fleishman, I.A. 2011. Twenty years of consideration and structure. In Fleishman, I.A. & Hunt, J.G. (eds.). Current Development in the Study of Leadership: Selected Readings, pp. 1-37. Carbondale: Southern Illinois University Press.`
- **Thesis**:
  `Howie, J. 2007. Topics in the theory of groupoids. Ph.D. Thesis, University of London.`
- **Electronic Resource**:
  `Clark, J.K. 2009. Humidity sensor. Journal of Physics 2(2): 9-13. http://www.cit.edu/phys/sensor.html [20 July 2009].`

## 7. Word Limits and Cover Colors

- **PhD / Professional Doctorate**: Max 100,000 words or 400 pages. Hard cover color: **Navy Blue**. Spine degree: `Dr. Fal.` or `D. P. P.`
- **Master by Research**: Max 60,000 words or 250 pages. Hard cover color: **Maroon**.
- **Master Coursework/Dissertation**: Max 30,000 words or 130 pages. Hard cover color: **Maroon**.
- **Postgraduate Diploma**: Hard cover color: **Black**.
