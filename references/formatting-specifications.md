# Gaya UKM Technical Formatting Specifications (2015 Edition)

This reference document compiles the exhaustive physical, mechanical, and typesetting measurements specified in **Chapter I (Technical Specifications)**, **Chapter II (Format)**, and relevant Appendices of the UKM Thesis Style Guide (*Gaya UKM 2015*).

---

## 1. Paper and Print Specifications (Section 1.1 - 1.3)

* **Paper Size**: A4 (`210 mm × 297 mm`). The bound volume must not deviate by more than `2 mm`.
* **Paper Quality**: Minimum `80 gsm` wood-free simile paper for the original submitted copy. High-clarity reproduction (photostat/offset minimum 600 dpi) for copies.
* **Printing Face**: Single-sided printing only. For Romanized script (English/Malay), the printed surface is on the right-hand side. For Arabic/Jawi script, the printed surface is on the left-hand side.

---

## 2. Typography and Font Hierarchy (Section 1.2)

* **Font Family**: **Times New Roman** exclusively throughout the entire thesis (Romanized script). For Arabic/Jawi script, use **Traditional Arabic**.
* **Font Sizes**:
  * **Title on Cover / Spine**: `18 pt`, all CAPITALS, gold embossed.
  * **Regular Body Text**: `12 pt`, justified alignment.
  * **Chapter Headings & Subheadings**: `11 pt`, bold (Chapter Title in CAPITALS).
  * **Table & Illustration Titles**: `10 pt`, bold.
  * **Table Contents & Figure Legends**: `10 pt`.
  * **Footnotes & Citations in Footnotes**: `10 pt`.
  * **Long Direct Quotations (>40 words)**: `12 pt`, single-spaced, indented `12.7 mm`.

---

## 3. Margins and Spacing Grid (Section 2.1)

### Standard Margins
* **Top Margin**: `30 mm` from the top of the paper.
* **Bottom Margin**: `25 mm` from the bottom edge.
* **Left Margin (Binding Side)**: `38 mm` from the left edge.
* **Right Margin**: `25 mm` from the right edge.

### Special Vertical Displacements
* **Chapter Opening Pages**: The heading `CHAPTER I` must begin **`50 mm`** from the top of the paper, centered.
* **References & Appendices Openings**: Heading begins **`30 mm`** from the top of the paper, centered.
* **Front Matter Headings** (`ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS`, `ABSTRACT`, `ABSTRAK`, `CONTENTS`, `LIST OF TABLES`): Centered, **`30 mm`** from top edge.
* **Cover Page Title**: First line of the inverted pyramid begins **`60 mm`** from the top edge.
* **Cover Page University Name**: `UNIVERSITI KEBANGSAAN MALAYSIA` sits **`60 mm`** from the bottom edge.
* **Title Page Submission Year**: Sits **`40 mm`** from the bottom edge (2 spaces below campus city name).

---

## 4. Spacing Rules (Section 1.2.4)

### 1.5 Line Spacing
* Applied to all general narrative body text in the thesis.

### 1.0 Single Line Spacing
Must be strictly applied to:
1. `DECLARATION`
2. `ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS`
3. `ABSTRAK` (Malay abstract)
4. `ABSTRACT` (English abstract)
5. `CONTENTS` (Table of contents entries)
6. `LIST OF TABLES` and `LIST OF ILLUSTRATIONS`
7. Text inside tables and captions
8. Long direct quotations (>40 words)
9. Footnotes and Endnotes
10. `REFERENCES` (Individual entries are single-spaced; separate entries by one blank line)

---

## 5. Paragraphing, Widows & Hyphenation (Section 1.2.5 & 2.1.1)

* **Paragraph Indentation**:
  * The **first paragraph** directly following any heading/subheading starts flush left at the margin (`0 mm` indent).
  * The **second and all subsequent paragraphs** must have their first line indented by **`12.7 mm` (0.5 inches)**.
* **Widows and Orphans Rule**:
  * The last paragraph on any page must have at least **two full printed lines**. If only one line fits, the entire sentence/clause must be moved to the top of the next page.
  * Every new page must contain at least **one line** of text before any new table/figure or section break.
* **Hyphenation Rule**:
  * The last word on the final line of a page **must never be broken with a hyphen**. The entire word must wrap to the next page.

---

## 6. Pagination System (Section 2.2)

* **Page Number Placement**:
  * **Top-right corner**, exactly **`15 mm`** from the top of the sheet.
  * The last digit of the page number must align **`25 mm`** from the right edge of the sheet.
  * Font: Same font as text (Times New Roman, regular, no brackets, hyphens, or adornments).
* **Preliminary Pages**:
  * Numbered in lower-case Roman numerals: `i, ii, iii, iv, v...`
  * Counting begins with the Title Page (page `i`), but the number **`i` is NOT printed**.
* **Body Text and Appendices**:
  * Numbered consecutively in Arabic numerals: `1, 2, 3, 4...`
  * Counting begins with the first page of Chapter I (page `1`), but the number **`1` is NOT printed**.
  * Similarly, the **first page of every subsequent chapter and major section** counts towards the sequence, but the page number **is suppressed and not printed**.

## 7. Submission Copies & Binding Specifications (Section 1.4 - 1.6)

### A. Examination Copy (Viva Voce)
* **Binding**: Normal soft-cover binding (glue bound / spiral bound as permitted by faculty).
* **Quantity**: Seven (7) copies submitted to the Faculty / Institute:
  1. External examiner
  2. Internal examiner
  3. Chair of the viva-voce examination
  4. Dean / Director of Faculty / Institute
  5. Chair of School / Head of Department
  6. Main supervisor
  7. Co-supervisor / supervisory committee member

### B. Final Approved Copy (Post-Viva Hard Cover)
* **Binding**: Heavy-board buckram hard cover with gold lettering embossed in capital letters.
* **Submission Quantity**: Minimum Two (2) hard copies (one original) + 1 CD-ROM containing the full PDF submitted to the Centre for Graduate Management, plus copies for the faculty/supervisors.
* **Cover Colors (Section 1.5.2)**:
  * **Doctor of Philosophy (PhD), DBA, DrPH, D.Litt, D.Med.Sc**: **Navy Blue** (Biru Tua)
  * **Master's Degree & Advanced Master**: **Maroon** (Merah Hati)
  * **Postgraduate Diploma**: **Black** (Hitam)

### C. Spine Specifications (Section 1.6.2 & Appendix C1 - C3)
Printed in gold capital letters (`18 pt`):
1. **Candidate Full Name**: `60 mm` from the top edge of the spine.
2. **Abbreviated Degree in Bahasa Melayu** (Appendix B):
   * PhD: `Dr. Fal.`
   * DBA: `D. P. P.`
   * Master of Business Administration: `S. P. P.`
   * Master of Economics: `S. Ekon.`
   * Master of Science: `S. Sn.`
   * Master of Arts: `S. Sa.`
3. **Year of Submission**.
4. **University Acronym**: `UKM`, typed `60 mm` from the bottom edge of the spine.
* *Note on thickness*:
  * Spine $> 25\text{ mm}$: text runs horizontally across spine width.
  * Spine $\le 25\text{ mm}$: text runs longitudinally along spine length.
