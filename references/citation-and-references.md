# Gaya UKM Citation and References Manual (2015 Edition)

This guide documents every citation rule from **Chapter IV (In Text Citations)** and **Chapter V (List of References)** of *Gaya UKM 2015*.

---

## Part 1: In-Text Citations (Chapter IV)

UKM mandates the **Author-Date system**.

### 1. The Core Parenthetical Rule (NO COMMA Rule)
* ❌ **Prohibited (APA Style)**: `(Reid, 2012)` or `(Jamaluddin, 2010)`
* ✅ **Mandatory (Gaya UKM)**: `(Reid 2012)` or `(Jamaluddin 2010)`
> **Rule**: There is **never a comma** between the author’s name and the publication year inside parenthetical citations.

### 2. Single Author (Section 4.2.1)
* **Narrative (Author in sentence)**:
  * `Reid (2012) demonstrated that...`
  * `As previously investigated by Romlah (2009)...`
  * `In the year 2012, Lokman discovered...` *(no parentheses needed if year is part of sentence)*
* **Parenthetical (Author outside sentence)**:
  * `...in teaching mathematics (Shaharir 2010).`
* **Repeated citation within the same paragraph**:
  * `In a study on mathematics education, Shaharir (2010) expressed the view that... Shaharir also observed that...` *(Year can be omitted if no ambiguity exists)*.

### 3. Co-Authors (Section 4.2.2)
* **Two Authors**:
  * In narrative text: use **`and`**
    * `Abdul Razak and Abu Osman (2009) proposed...`
    * `Baharudin and Abdullah Marwi (2012) identified...`
  * In parenthetical citation: use **`&`**
    * `...as previously verified (Abdul Rahman & Arsmah 2009).`
* **Three or More Authors**:
  * Mention only the first author followed by **`et al.`** and year:
  * Narrative: `Shafaratul et al. (2011) provided a detailed...`
  * Parenthetical: `...reduced DNA deterioration (Siti Balkis et al. 2011).`

### 4. Organizations or Institutions as Authors (Section 4.2.3)
* First mention:
  * `(Department of Occupational Safety and Health [DOSH] 2010)` or `Department of Occupational Safety and Health (DOSH 2010)`
  * `(Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka [DBP] 2011)`
* Subsequent mentions:
  * `(DOSH 2010)` or `DBP (2011)`

### 5. Unknown Authors (Section 4.2.4)
* Use `Anon.` (Anonymous):
  * `Anon. (2012)` or `(Anon. 2012)`

### 6. Multiple Citations in One Place (Section 4.2.6)
* **Multiple Different Authors**:
  * Order alphabetically by first author's surname.
  * Separate citations with semicolons (`;`):
    ```text
    (Abdul Samad 2011; Azahan 2011; Jamaluddin 2010; Kadir 2012)
    ```
* **Same Author, Multiple Years**:
  * Separate years with commas:
    ```text
    (Jamaluddin 2007, 2009, 2012)
    ```
* **Same Author, Same Year**:
  * Distinguish with lowercase letters:
    ```text
    (Sham 2010a, 2010b)
    ```

## Part 2: Reference List Conventions (Chapter V)

### General Typesetting Specifications
1. **Heading**: `REFERENCES` centered, `30 mm` from top edge, uppercase bold (`11 pt`).
2. **Line Spacing**: Single-spaced within each reference entry.
3. **Paragraph Hanging Indent**: **`12.5 mm`** on the second and subsequent lines of each entry.
4. **Spacing Between Entries**: One blank line (single space) between entries.
5. **Alphabetical Ordering**: Strict A-Z order by author surname/corporate name.
   * *Special Exception*: `Al-Quran` always heads the reference list before `A`.
6. **No Parentheses Around Publication Year**:
   * Format: `Author. Year.` (e.g., `Cohen, J. 2009.`), never `Cohen, J. (2009).`

## Part 3: Author Name Rules by Ethnicity & Nationality (Section 5.1.3)

| Origin | Rule | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Western** | Surname first, followed by comma and initials. | • Humphrey John Moule Bowen $\rightarrow$ `Bowen, H.J.M.`<br>• John Fitzgerald Kennedy $\rightarrow$ `Kennedy, J.F.` |
| **Malay** | Full name written direct (no inversion), initials follow if abbreviated. | • Noor Azlan Ghazali $\rightarrow$ `Noor Azlan Ghazali. 2012.`<br>• Zakri A. H. $\rightarrow$ `Zakri, A.H. 2008.`<br>• H.M. Dahlan $\rightarrow$ `Dahlan, H.M. 2006.` |
| **Chinese** | Surname first followed by given name. If Christian/English name is present, initials follow. | • Ding Choo Ming $\rightarrow$ `Ding, Choo Ming. 2008.`<br>• H.S. Lee $\rightarrow$ `Lee, H.S. 2011.`<br>• Edward Lim Huck Tee $\rightarrow$ `Lim, E.H.T. 2005.` |
| **Indian** | Surname or given name with initials following. | • S. Bhaskaran $\rightarrow$ `Bhaskaran, S. 2010.`<br>• K. Sarvananthan $\rightarrow$ `Sarvananthan, K. 2009.` |
| **Arabic** | Family name / well-known name first. | • Sulayman ibn Ahmad al-Tabrani $\rightarrow$ `al-Tabrani, Sulayman ibn Ahmad.`<br>• Ibn Taymiyyah $\rightarrow$ `Ibn Taymiyyah, Taqi al-Din.` |
| **Sabah / Sarawak Native** | Direct full name as published. | • James anak Bachat $\rightarrow$ `James Bachat. 2010.` |

## Part 4: Specific Reference Templates & Official UKM Examples

### 1. Journal Articles (Section 5.5)
**Formula**:
> Author. Year. Article title. *Journal Title* Volume(Issue): Pages.

* **Single Author**:
  ```text
  Samad, S.A. 2004. Digital domain design of cascaded wave digital filters with tunable parameters. Iranian Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering 3(2): 1-5.
  ```
* **Multiple Authors**:
  ```text
  Andanastuti Muchtar, Lim, L.C. & Lee, K.H. 2002. Finite element analysis of Vickers indentation cracking processes in brittle solids using elements exhibiting cohesive post failure behaviour. Journal of Materials Science 38(2): 235-243.

  Hamzah, N., Mohamed, A. & Hussain, A. 2004. A new approach to locate the voltage sag source using real current component. Journal of Electric Power System Research 72: 113-123.
  ```
* **Abbreviated Journal Name**:
  ```text
  Brown, R. & Higgins, P.J. 1978. On the connection between the second relative homotopy groups of some related spaces. Proc. London Math. Soc. 36(3): 193-212.
  ```

---

### 2. Books (Section 5.3)
**Formula**:
> Author. Year. *Book Title*. Edition (if not 1st). Place of publication: Publisher.

* **Standard Book**:
  ```text
  Smith, M., Beck, J., Cooper, C.L., Cow, C., Ottaway, D. & Talbot, R. 2008. Introducing Organizational Behaviour. London: MacMillan.
  ```
* **New Editions / Reprints**:
  ```text
  Cohen, J. 2009. Statistical Power Analysis for the Behavioral Sciences. Revised Ed. New York: Academic Press.

  Buckley, C.B. 2006. An Anecdotal History of Old Times in Singapore. Reprint. Kuala Lumpur: University of Malaya Press.
  ```
* **Edited Book (ed. / eds.)**:
  ```text
  Jamaluddin Md. Jahi (ed.). 2010. Pengurusan Persekitaran di Malaysia: Isu dan Cabaran. Bangi: Pusat Pengajian Siswazah, Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia.

  Jamaluddin Md. Jahi, Kamaruzzaman Sopian, Mohd Jailani Mohd Nor & Abdul Hadi Harman Shah (eds.). 2011. Environmental Management 2000. Bangi: Centre for Graduate Studies, Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia.
  ```

---

### 3. Chapter in an Edited Book (Section 5.3.11)
**Formula**:
> Author. Year. Chapter title. In Editor Name (ed.). *Book Title*, pp. Pages. Place of publication: Publisher.

* **Examples**:
  ```text
  Fleishman, I.A. 2011. Twenty years of consideration and structure. In Fleishman, I.A. & Hunt, J.G. (eds.). Current Development in the Study of Leadership: Selected Readings, pp. 1-37. Carbondale: Southern Illinois University Press.

  Abdul Latiff Mohamad. 2000. Kesan pembangunan sosio-ekonomi ke atas kepelbagaian biologi khususnya spesies tumbuhan endemik di Malaysia. In Jamaluddin Md. Jahi (ed.). Pengurusan Persekitaran di Malaysia: Isu dan Cabaran, pp. 72-103. Bangi: Pusat Pengajian Siswazah, Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia.
  ```

---

### 4. Conference Proceedings (Section 5.6)
**Formula**:
> Author. Year. Article title. *Proceedings of Conference*, pp. Pages.

* **Examples**:
  ```text
  Rahmat, R.A., Jumari, K., Hassan, A. & Basri, H. 2002. Intelligent traffic control with image processing sensor. Proceedings of the 2nd World Engineering Congress, pp. 339-344.

  Kadir Arifin, Jamaluddin Md. Jahi, Abu Bakar Che Man, Ismail Bahari & Abd Rahim Md Nor. 2003. Perlaksanaan sistem pengurusan keselamatan dan kesihatan pekerjaan (OHSAS 18001:1999) di Malaysia: Isu dan cabaran. Prosiding Pengurusan Persekitaran 2003, pp. 107-115.
  ```

---

### 5. Dissertations and Theses (Section 5.12.1)
**Formula**:
> Author. Year. Title of thesis. Degree Type (Ph.D. Thesis / Master Thesis), Department/Faculty, University Name.

* **Examples**:
  ```text
  Howie, J. 2007. Topics in the theory of groupoids. Ph.D. Thesis, University of London.

  Mann, A.E. 2006. The palaeodemography of Australopithecus. Ph.D. Dissertation, University of California, Berkeley.

  Alfitra Salam. 1999. Kerjasama dan potensi konflik dalam hubungan Indonesia-Malaysia 1966-1991. Ph.D. Thesis, Jabatan Sejarah, Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia.
  ```

---

### 6. Public / Government Documents and Acts (Section 5.4)
* **Acts of Parliament**:
  ```text
  Malaysia. 1990. Bernama Act 1967 (Revised 1990). (Act 449).
  Malaysia. 1974. Environmental Quality Act. (Act 127).
  ```
* **Government Plans & Reports**:
  ```text
  Malaysia. 2006. Ninth Malaysia Plan 2006-2010. Putrajaya: Economic Planning Unit.
  Ministry of Finance Malaysia. 2001. Economic report 2001/2002. pp. 63-77.
  ```
* **International Organizations**:
  ```text
  WHO. 1991. Guidelines for the Assessment of Herbal Medicines. Geneva: World Health Organization.
  UNESCO. 1960. Director-General of UNESCO Report. Paris: UNESCO.
  ```

---

### 7. Electronic Resources and Websites (Section 5.13)
**Formula**:
> Author / Organization. Year. Title. *Source Title* (if available) Volume(Issue): Pages. URL [Date of access].

* **Examples**:
  ```text
  Clark, J.K. 2009. Humidity sensor. Journal of Physics 2(2): 9-13. http://www.cit.edu/phys/sensor.html [20 July 2009].

  Crane, N. 2007. Electronic sources: MLA style of citation. http://www.uvm.edu/ncrane/estyle/mla.html [31 July 2007].

  Medical Information Group. 2008. Arsenic contamination of drinking water. http://www.cit.edu/phys/sensor.html [4 July 2008].
  ```
*(Note: Do not underline URLs; always wrap the access date in square brackets followed by a period).*

### 8. Newspaper and Magazine Articles (Section 5.7 - 5.8)
* **Newspaper with Author**:
  ```text
  Gunasegaran, P. 2006. Overworked students: give the kids chance to relax. New Straits Times, 21 February: 20.
  ```
* **Newspaper without Author (`Anon.`)**:
  ```text
  Anon. 2007. MUET gets a fresh look. The Star, 16 March.
  ```
* **Magazine Article**:
  ```text
  Fergus Bordewich. 2005. Top of the class. Reader's Digest September: 58-64.
  ```
