# UKM Postgraduate Academic Regulations & Policies (2026 Edition)

This statutory reference document details the policies, graduation criteria, examination procedures, and submission bylaws set forth in the **Kaedah-Kaedah Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (Pengajian Pascasiswazah) 2026** and **Peraturan-Peraturan Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (Pengajian Pascasiswazah) 2026**, effective from the 2026/2027 Academic Session.

---

## 1. Statutory Framework & Scope

* **Legislative Authority**: Enacted under the Constitution of Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (*Perlembagaan Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia*).
* **Target Programs**: Doctor of Philosophy (PhD), Professional Doctorates (DBA, DrPH, EdD, etc.), Master's by Research, Mixed-Mode Master's, and Master's by Coursework.
* **Governing Bodies**: Senate of UKM (*Senat*), Faculty / Institute Graduate Studies Committee (*JKPP*), and the Centre for Graduate Management (*Pusat Pengurusan Pascasiswazah*).

---

## 2. Mandatory Publication Requirements for Graduation (Peraturan 25)

To graduate, postgraduate research candidates must fulfill strict minimum publication quotas verified prior to degree conferral. The UKM supervisor **must be listed as co-author** with UKM institutional affiliation.

### A. Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)

| Discipline / Cluster | Minimum Publication Prerequisite | Indexing Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Science, Technology, and Medicine** | Minimum **TWO (2) journal articles** | Indexed in **Web of Science (WoS) / Scopus** |
| **Social Sciences and Humanities** | Minimum **TWO (2) journal articles** OR<br>Minimum **ONE (1) WoS/Scopus article** + **ONE (1) article** in a recognized UKM / MyCite journal or peer-reviewed book chapter | **WoS / Scopus** + UKM/MyCite/Book Chapter |

### B. Master's Degree (Penyelidikan / Research Mode)

| Discipline / Cluster | Minimum Publication Prerequisite | Indexing Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Science, Technology, and Medicine** | Minimum **ONE (1) journal article** | Indexed in **Web of Science (WoS) / Scopus** |
| **Social Sciences and Humanities** | Minimum **ONE (1) journal article** OR<br>Minimum **ONE (1) article** in a recognized UKM journal / MyCite / peer-reviewed book chapter | **ERA / WoS / Scopus** OR UKM/MyCite/Book Chapter |

### C. Master's Degree (Kerja Kursus / Mod Campuran - Coursework / Mixed Mode)
* Candidates must fulfill research project publication or presentation requirements specified by the respective Faculty/Institute.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Timeline of Publication Proof (Peraturan 24 & 25)**:
> 1. **At Thesis Submission (Viva Notice)**: Candidate must submit official proof of manuscript submission (*bukti penghantaran manuskrip*).
> 2. **Before Viva Voce / Degree Award**: Candidate must submit official letter of acceptance (*surat penerimaan / accepted*) or published print from the indexed journal.

## 3. Thesis Submission Protocols (Peraturan 24)

### A. Three (3) Months Advance Notice of Submission (*Notis Serahan Tesis*)
* A candidate must submit the official **Notice of Thesis/Dissertation Submission** (*Borang Notis Serahan Tesis/Disertasi*) to the Dean / Director **at least three (3) months** before submitting the final examination copies.
* Failure to provide 3 months advance notice may delay the appointment of external examiners and postpone the oral examination (*viva voce*).

### B. Anti-Plagiarism Certification (*Pengesahan Bebas Plagiat*)
* Before examination copies are accepted by the Faculty:
  1. The thesis manuscript must undergo automated similarity screening (e.g., Turnitin).
  2. The candidate's supervisor and Faculty Dean/Director must issue an official **Certificate of Plagiarism-Free Status** (*Pengesahan Bebas Plagiat*).
  3. The similarity index must remain strictly within UKM's institutional threshold (typically $\le 20\%$, excluding references, preliminary pages, and quotations).

## 4. Dual Thesis Formats (Peraturan 23)

UKM formally recognizes two distinct structural formats for Master's dissertations and PhD theses:

### A. Conventional Monograph Format (*Format Konvensional*)
* The classical five-to-six chapter monograph (Chapter I: Introduction, Chapter II: Literature Review, Chapter III: Methodology, Chapter IV: Results, Chapter V: Discussion, Chapter VI: Conclusion).
* Adheres strictly to the sequential flow defined in *Gaya UKM 2015*.

### B. Compilation Format (*Format Kompilasi* / Thesis by Publication)
* Incorporates multiple peer-reviewed journal papers (published or accepted during the candidature) into the thesis.
* **Language Exemption**: Candidates electing the Compilation Format are **automatically permitted to write in English** without requiring a special waiver from the Senate/Graduate Committee.
* **Structural Integration Requirements**:
  1. Must contain an overarching **General Introduction** contextualizing all studies.
  2. Must contain an overarching **Literature Review** connecting the thematic framework.
  3. Paper chapters must be linked with narrative bridges detailing how each study builds upon preceding findings.
  4. Must conclude with a unified **General Discussion and Comprehensive Conclusion** summarizing overall theoretical, practical, and methodological contributions.

## 5. Maximum Word Count Limits (Peraturan 23(6)-(7))

Word limits apply to the core body text (from Chapter 1 Introduction through to the final Conclusion chapter):

| Degree Program | Candidature Mode | Word Limit (Maximum) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)** | Research (*Penyelidikan*) | **100,000 words** |
| **Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)** | Mixed Mode (*Mod Campuran*) | **60,000 words** |
| **Master's Degree** | Research (*Penyelidikan*) | **60,000 words** |
| **Master's Degree** | Coursework / Mixed Mode (*Kerja Kursus / Campuran*) | **50,000 words** *(increased from 30,000 in 2015)* |

> [!NOTE]
> **Exclusions from Word Count**: Preliminary pages (Title, Declaration, Acknowledgements, Abstracts, TOC), References/Bibliography, Footnotes/Endnotes, Tables, Figure Captions, and Appendices.
> **Exceeding Limits**: If a thesis exceeds these caps, formal written permission must be obtained from the Dean/Director at least **3 months** prior to submission.

## 6. Examination, Viva Voce & Corrections (Kaedah 21 & 25)

### A. Examination Committee Composition (Kaedah 19 & 20)
* Chairperson appointed by Senate/Dean.
* At least **One (1) External Examiner** (*Pemeriksa Luar*) of professorial or senior academic standing.
* At least **One (1) Internal Examiner** (*Pemeriksa Dalam*).
* Faculty / Graduate Studies Representative.
* Main supervisor attending in observer/advisory capacity.

### B. Oral Examination (*Viva Voce*) Decisions (Kaedah 21)
The viva voce board classifies the candidate into one of the following official outcomes:

1. **Category 1 - Pass without Corrections** (*Lulus tanpa pembetulan*): Degree awarded immediately.
2. **Category 2 - Pass with Minor Corrections** (*Lulus dengan pembetulan kecil*): Corrections completed within **1 to 3 months**, verified by the supervisor and/or internal examiner.
3. **Category 3 - Pass with Major Corrections** (*Lulus dengan pembetulan besar*):
   - Correction period allowed: **6 to 12 months**.
   - If 12 months corrections are prescribed, the candidate must undergo a **Re-examination / Repeat Viva Voce** (*Viva Ulangan*).
   - Post-viva repeat corrections: Candidate has maximum **3 months** to submit revisions, with an emergency extension capped at 3 months.
4. **Category 4 - Resubmission for Re-examination** (*Tolak dengan kebenaran serah semula*): Candidate must rework the thesis and re-submit within 12 to 18 months.
5. **Category 5 - Fail** (*Gagal*): Candidature terminated.

### C. Distinction Award: *Tesis Cemerlang* (Kaedah 25)
A candidate may be nominated and awarded the prestigious *Tesis Cemerlang* (Thesis with Distinction) if:
1. Both internal and external examiners unanimously grade the thesis as exceptional.
2. The candidate significantly exceeds minimum publication benchmarks (e.g., top-tier WoS Q1/Q2 publications).
3. The degree is completed within the stipulated Graduate On Time (**GOT**) duration:
   * **PhD**: $\le 8$ semesters (Full-Time) / $\le 10$ semesters (Part-Time).
   * **Master's**: $\le 6$ semesters (Full-Time) / $\le 8$ semesters (Part-Time).

### D. Flexible Exit Route (*Keluar Fleksibel* - Kaedah 22)
* If a PhD candidate's thesis does not fulfill the rigorous doctoral standards but meets the academic criteria for a Master's degree, the Viva Voce Examination Board may recommend conferring a Master's degree by Research.

## 7. Checklist for UKM Postgraduate Thesis Submission

Candidates preparing for thesis submission must ensure compliance with both Gaya UKM formatting and the 2026 Postgraduate Regulations:

- [ ] **3-Month Notice**: Submitted *Borang Notis Serahan Tesis* 3 months in advance.
- [ ] **Publication Compliance**: Met WoS/Scopus journal quotas (PhD: 2; Master Research: 1) with UKM supervisor co-authorship.
- [ ] **Similarity Clearance**: Obtained official *Pengesahan Bebas Plagiat* certificate (Turnitin).
- [ ] **Word Count Check**: Verified body word count within prescribed ceilings (PhD: 100k; Master: 60k/50k).
- [ ] **Format Alignment**: Selected Conventional Monograph or Compilation Format.
- [ ] **Gaya UKM Layout**: 38 mm binding margin, inverted pyramid title, 1-paragraph single-spaced abstract (200-350 words, no citations), 3-line tables, TNR 12 pt / 1.5 line spacing.
