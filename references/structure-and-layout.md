# Gaya UKM Structure and Layout Guidelines (2015 Edition)

This manual provides the structural blueprints, page templates, and sectioning rules outlined in **Chapter III (Layout and Content)** of the official UKM Thesis Style Guide.

---

## 1. Mandatory Sequence of the Thesis (Section 3.1)

A thesis or dissertation must assemble all components in the exact following chronological order:

```text
1.  TITLE PAGE (in language of thesis)
2.  TRANSLATION OF TITLE PAGE (Bahasa Melayu translation for English thesis)
3.  DECLARATION (Pengakuan)
4.  ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS (Penghargaan)
5.  ABSTRAK (Malay Abstract)
6.  ABSTRACT (English Abstract)
7.  CONTENTS (Kandungan / Table of Contents)
8.  LIST OF TABLES (Senarai Jadual)
9.  LIST OF ILLUSTRATIONS (Senarai Ilustrasi / Rajah)
10. LIST OF SYMBOLS / ABBREVIATIONS / GLOSSARY (Senarai Simbol / Singkatan)
11. LIST OF CASES (Senarai Kes - if applicable)
12. LIST OF STATUTES (Senarai Statut - if applicable)
13. BODY OF THE THESIS (Teks: Chapters I to V/VI)
14. REFERENCES (Rujukan)
15. APPENDICES (Lampiran)
```

---

## 2. Preliminary Page Blueprints

### A. Title Page (Section 3.2 & Appendix D1, D3)
* **Spacing**: Single-spaced, all CAPITALS.
* **Layout**:
  * **Thesis Title**: Arranged in an **inverted pyramid (piramid terbalik)**, beginning **`60 mm`** from the top edge.
  * **Candidate Full Name**: As shown on passport/national ID card.
  * **Degree Statement**:
    ```text
    THESIS SUBMITTED IN FULFILMENT OF THE REQUIREMENTS FOR
    THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF PHILOSOPHY
    ```
    *(For Master coursework/dissertation: `DISSERTATION SUBMITTED IN PARTIAL FULFILMENT...`)*
  * **Faculty / Institute**:
    ```text
    FACULTY OF ECONOMICS AND MANAGEMENT
    UNIVERSITI KEBANGSAAN MALAYSIA
    BANGI
    ```
  * **Submission Date**: Month and Year (e.g., `OCTOBER 2026`), positioned **`40 mm`** from the bottom edge.
* **Malay Translation Title Page (Appendix D3)**:
  Directly follows the English title page with identical layout in Bahasa Melayu:
  * Title in Malay (Inverted pyramid)
  * Degree statement: `TESIS DIKEMUKAKAN UNTUK MEMENUHI SYARAT MEMPEROLEH IJAZAH DOKTOR FALSAFAH`
  * `FAKULTI EKONOMI DAN PENGURUSAN, UNIVERSITI KEBANGSAAN MALAYSIA, BANGI`

---

### B. Declaration Page (Section 3.3 & Appendix E1)
Must be typed on a new page, single-spaced, centered:

```text
DECLARATION

I hereby declare that the work in this thesis is my own except for
quotations and summaries which have been duly acknowledged.


[Date]                                      [Candidate's Signature]
                                            [FULL NAME OF CANDIDATE]
                                            [MATRIC NO: Pxxxxxx]
```

---

### C. Acknowledgements (Section 3.4 & Appendix F1)
* Heading `ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS` centered, **`30 mm`** from top edge.
* Single-spaced throughout.
* Explicitly acknowledge funding/grants, university support, supervisors, and contributors.

---

### D. Abstracts (Section 3.5 & Appendix G1 - G4)
* **Language Order for English Thesis**:
  1. `ABSTRAK` (Malay translated version) appears first.
  2. `ABSTRACT` (English version) follows on the next page.
* **Length**: Strictly between **200 and 350 words**.
* **Structure**: Exactly **one single paragraph**, **single-spaced**.
* **Required Core Contents**:
  1. Statement of the problem / Research questions
  2. Aim and primary objectives of the study
  3. Study context / area (if relevant)
  4. Research methodology (design, instruments, sampling, data analysis)
  5. Major findings and empirical results
  6. Practical, theoretical, or managerial contributions

---

### E. Table of Contents & Lists (Section 3.6 - 3.11 & Appendix H - M)
* **Table of Contents (TOC)**:
  * Headings are single-spaced within an item, with double spacing between major divisions.
  * Dot leaders connect headings to page numbers flush right.
  * **CRITICAL RULE (Sec 3.12.5)**: **Subheadings deeper than level 3 (e.g. 1.1.1.1 or a, b, c) are STRICTLY FORBIDDEN from appearing in the Table of Contents.** Only Chapters (Level 1), First-level headings (Level 2: `1.1`), and Second-level headings (Level 3: `1.1.1`) are permitted.
* **Lists of Tables / Illustrations**:
  * Titles must match word-for-word the titles displayed on the actual pages.
  * Numbered according to chapters: `Table 3.1`, `Figure 4.2`.

## 3. Heading Hierarchy and Sectioning (Section 3.12.5)

UKM enforces a maximum 5-level sectioning architecture:

```text
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Level 1: CHAPTER I           [Centered, BOLD, 11 pt, 50 mm from top margin]
         INTRODUCTION        [Centered, BOLD, 11 pt, 2 spaces below Chapter No.]

Level 2: 1.1  BACKGROUND    [Flush left, BOLD, 11 pt, 2 spaces below Chapter Title]

Level 3: 1.1.1  Industry     [Flush left, BOLD, 11 pt, 2 decimal points max]
                Overview

Level 4: a.  Upstream Segment [Flush left, Regular/Bold, 11 pt, Not in TOC]

Level 5: i.  Refining Costs  [Flush left, Regular, 11 pt, Not in TOC]
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

> **Rules**:
> 1. Never number beyond the third level using decimal digits (e.g. `1.1.1.1` is invalid in UKM).
> 2. Fourth level must use alphabets: `a., b., c.`
> 3. Fifth level must use lowercase Roman numerals: `i., ii., iii.`
> 4. Subtopics beyond the fifth level are completely disallowed.

## 4. Tables and Illustrations (Section 3.12.6)

### A. Tables
* **Caption Position**: **ABOVE** the table.
  ```text
  Table 4.2  Summary of Cronbach's Alpha Reliability Analysis
  ```
* **Font Size**: **`10 pt`** for table title, headers, and cell contents.
* **Grid Formatting (Appendix P1 - P3)**:
  * Classic **three-line table** (Top boundary rule, table header underline, bottom table boundary rule).
  * **No vertical interior lines**.
* **Landscape Tables**:
  * The top (head) of the table must align along the **left (bound) edge** of the page.
* **Multi-Page Tables**:
  * Table header row must repeat on subsequent pages.
  * Above the repeated header, place: `Table 4.2 (continued)`.

### B. Illustrations / Figures
* **Caption Position**: **BELOW** the figure.
  ```text
  Figure 3.1  Structural Equation Modeling Path Coefficients
  ```
* **Font Size**: **`10 pt`** for figure caption and internal labels.
* **Text Precedence Rule**:
  * Discussion of the table or figure in the text **must precede** the appearance of the graphic itself (e.g., `As illustrated in Figure 3.1...`).

---

## 5. Direct Quotations (Section 3.12.7)

* **Short Quotations ($\le 40$ words)**:
  * Kept within the body paragraph.
  * Enclosed in double quotation marks: `"..."`.
* **Long Quotations ($> 40$ words)**:
  * Extracted into a freestanding block paragraph.
  * Formatted with **1.0 single line spacing**.
  * Separated from surrounding text by 1.5 line spaces above and below.
  * Indented **`12.7 mm` (0.5 in)** from the left margin.
  * **DO NOT USE QUOTATION MARKS**.
* **Foreign Language Quotations**:
  * Any quotation in a language other than the primary thesis language must be formatted in *italics*.
